WHAT IT MUST SHOW
A takes stack opens over the board: Take 3 of 5, each titled by its angle, with a size readout and a filmstrip.
Single and Grid views. The take in the viewer is live, not a picture.
Keep on board (Enter) adds the take as a draft. The accepted baseline never changes until a review accepts something.
Holding B fades the take to the baseline. Compare shows baseline r8 beside the take with changed regions outlined and numbered.
Unfinished and failed takes are marked and cannot be kept. Cancel and Retry are always in reach.
The exact inputs of the run are listed: frame, style and requirement revisions. The whole board is never sent.
After Keep, a toast names the new draft and offers Undo.
MOTION
Stack opens
The viewer scales in from the stack card in 220 ms with a small overshoot (Onlook bubble timing); the scrim fades in 160 ms.
Step
Take to take is a 160 ms cross-fade. The filmstrip scrolls the active thumbnail into view in 250 ms.
Hold B
The take fades to the baseline in 90 ms and back in 120 ms. No state is left behind when the key is released.
Keep
The take moves to its place on the board in 250 ms; the toast rises 8 px over 240 ms (Momo reveal timing).
Generating
Unfinished takes breathe on a 2.8 s cycle. A finished take replaces its copy with a 160 ms cross-fade.
Reduce Motion
Fades and moves become instant, breathing stops, and the toast stays 6 s so it can be read.
