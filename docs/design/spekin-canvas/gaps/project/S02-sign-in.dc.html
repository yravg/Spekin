Syarat Penggunaan

Bahasa IndonesiaBerlaku sejak 1 Des 2026 (contoh)·Teks contoh, belum ditinjau hukum
01

Tentang layanan

Spekin membantu Anda menyusun spesifikasi produk dari sebuah ide, lewat wawancara singkat, log keputusan, dan dokumen spesifikasi.

02

Akun Anda

Anda masuk dengan email atau akun Google. Jaga kode masuk Anda dan jangan bagikan kepada orang lain.

03

Konten Anda

Ide, jawaban, dan dokumen yang Anda buat tetap milik Anda. Kami hanya memprosesnya untuk menjalankan layanan.

04

Penggunaan yang dilarang

Jangan memakai Spekin untuk melanggar hukum atau mengunggah data pribadi orang lain tanpa izin.

05

Kredit dan biaya

Pekerjaan berbayar menampilkan perkiraan biaya sebelum dijalankan. Anda boleh menolaknya.

06

Perubahan syarat

Jika syarat berubah, kami memberi tahu Anda di aplikasi sebelum syarat baru berlaku.

English

At launch both pages are in Bahasa Indonesia only. An English text follows later.

Back to sign-in

EN selected, Bahasa shown: the page does not translate by itself. It says so in one line and keeps the Bahasa text.
Data table: what is kept, why, and for how long, in the user’s own language.
What it must show
  • Sign-in offers the email code first and Google second. Both are on one card and both reach the same account for the same address.
  • No password is ever set. The email step asks for one address and nothing else.
  • Code entry: six boxes, paste fills all, a resend countdown, and a limit of 3 wrong tries before the code is voided.
  • Errors say what happened and what to do next. They never reveal whether an email already has an account.
  • Syarat and Privasi are linked in the top bar and in the sign-in card, and open real pages in Bahasa Indonesia, never a 404.
  • At launch the legal pages are Bahasa only. With EN selected a one-line notice says so; the text is not machine translated.
  • The language switch (EN, ID) is on every public screen. Bahasa Indonesia is the default.
Motion
Card stepEmail step swaps to the code step in place: 160 ms cross-fade with a 4 px slide (spring-ui).
Code boxesFocus ring 120 ms. A wrong code shakes the boxes 2 px three times inside 240 ms.
ResendThe countdown is text that ticks once a second. No spinner without a real progress value.
Google hand-offThe button text changes to “Waiting for Google” with a 140 ms fade.
Legal pagesThe contents list scrolls the page 200 ms and keeps the current item underlined.
Reduce MotionAll swaps are instant and the shake is replaced by the red boxes and the message.

Where it lives
Route: /masuk, /syarat, /privasi. The route names are TO CONFIRM (AC-43). The legal pages need no session.UI (TS/React): yravg UI text-input, otp-input, button as the PRD maps S02. No OpenDesign code is reused here.Auth: Better Auth with email code and Google, not Google only (PRD 6.1). The server owns code expiry (10 minutes), the tries limit and rate limits; the screen only shows state.Open for Enrico: legal review of the Bahasa text · the English text date · route names · whether the landing page links the same two pages.
