NEW
Start from a requirement: accepted spec r14 lists three requirements with no frame. Clicking one drafts a frame for it and nothing else.
Draw a frame (F): drag on the board and a live size readout shows 1280 × 900. On release Spekin asks for a prompt or a reference. The frame is a draft until it is reviewed.
What it must show
  • An infinite board of frames. Zoom scales the frames; tools, titles, chips and handles keep their size on screen.
  • Tool palette: select, pan, frame, text, nitpick pin, import. Each has a key, and every drag action has a non-drag route (frame list, keyboard).
  • View menu and zoom control: fit, fit selection, 100%, zoom in, zoom out, with their keys.
  • Each frame shows its title, revision chip, Export and more. Accepted, draft and reference frames look different.
  • The prompt bar names its scope (board, one frame, N frames) and lists exactly what the run gets. Earlier chats wait behind it.
  • Selection uses counter-scaled rings and handles. A selection that is off screen is marked at the edge, with a Recenter button.
  • Frame covers show designing, waiting and failed with Retry. The top bar counts designing and waiting jobs and notes when capacity is full.
  • Empty, zoomed-out and busy boards are drawn, not left to chance.
Motion
CameraFit, Fit selection and Recenter move the camera in 250 ms (Onlook). Opening a board is instant. Zoom steps use the same ease.
SelectionRing and handles fade in over 120 ms. Their size is counter-scaled, so they never change on screen.
Chats behind the barDeal out with a 40 ms stagger, 240 ms each, ease cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1) (OpenDesign); fold back in 160 ms.
Frame coverThe designing ground breathes on a 2.8 s cycle, opacity .5 to 1. Waiting and failed covers are still.
Busy chipThe dot pulses on a 1.6 s cycle while any job runs. The count text swaps with a 140 ms fade.
Reduce MotionCamera moves jump, the breathing ground and the pulse stop, chats appear without a stagger. Status text still changes.
