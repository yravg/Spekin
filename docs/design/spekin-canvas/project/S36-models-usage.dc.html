Top up credits

Pay by QRIS through a payment adapter. Credits are added once, after the payment is verified. Automatic top-up stays off. Pack sizes and prices are not decided yet.

At the cap Send is off and says why. A second tab or a retry cannot sneak a run in: the reserve is checked atomically.
Top up is drawn but off (LATER). Automatic top-up is never on without a separate approval.
What it must show
  • Three sources, each saying who bills: Hosted (Spekin credits), BYOK (your provider), Local agent (shown off and tagged LATER).
  • Provider chips and a model radio group. Each model shows capabilities, limits, rate card and retention; anything not known says unknown. A model that cannot be used is disabled with the reason.
  • Effort, and Fixed or Auto routing. Auto cannot switch provider or pass the per-run cap on its own, and every run names the model it used.
  • The BYOK key is write-only: encrypted on the server, shown only while typed, never again. A connection test lists what passed, and Revoke is one click away.
  • Before Send: the estimate range, the exact context in tokens, the checks, and the maximum that will be reserved. After the run: settle the actual, refund the rest.
  • A usage meter with reserved and pending pieces, calm until 70%, warn at 70%, critical at 90%; caps per run and per month; rate windows.
  • At the cap new hosted runs stop, the limit is named, and every artifact stays. Credit top-up is drawn and off (LATER).
Motion
Meter fillBar width eases 320 ms spring-panel. Colour changes at 70% and 90% with a 140 ms fade; nothing pulses.
EstimateThe range band redraws in 180 ms when model, effort or context changes; numbers swap with a 120 ms fade.
Source switchThe form below cross-fades 200 ms spring-ui. Typed key text is cleared on a switch, never carried over.
Ledger rowsNew entries rise 4 px with a 40 ms stagger, ease cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1) (OpenDesign).
Connection testRows resolve one by one every 60 ms. A spinner shows only while that row’s request is open; no fake progress bar.
Reduce MotionFills and swaps are instant. Severity and cap messages are still announced as text.
