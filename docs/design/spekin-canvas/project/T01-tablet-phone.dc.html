44Every target is 44 × 44 px or larger, with 8 px between neighbours.

What it must show
  • Below 1024 px the free canvas is replaced by a frame list and a single-frame viewer. Review and hand off stay fully available; canvas editing says it is on desktop.
  • Tablet (768 to 1023 px): the list sits beside one frame. Context and review open in place of the list, never stacked beside the viewer.
  • Phone (under 768 px): a frame list, then one frame, then sheets. Tabs for Frames, Style, Review and Hand off sit at the bottom, in thumb reach.
  • Every target is 44 px or larger. Actions are always visible: no hover, no hold, no drag. A bottom sheet replaces a popover or a context menu.
  • Safe areas stay clear at the top and bottom. Text is 16 px so the browser never zooms into a field.
  • Review shows Original and Changed as a toggle, then Dismiss and Apply change always in view; Apply asks first and Cancel is the default.
  • Hand off lists accepted revisions only, shows why a draft is left out, and builds one fixed snapshot with a ready sheet or a Retry.
Motion
Bottom sheetRises 220 ms ease cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1) with a scrim fade of 160 ms. It closes by Cancel, a scrim tap or a swipe down.
Panel swapOn a tablet the review panel replaces the list in 200 ms spring-ui; the viewer keeps its frame and zoom.
Frame to frameThe viewer cross-fades 160 ms; the list keeps its scroll position. No reload.
Tab changeThe bottom tab marker slides 200 ms; the page does not reload and never approves anything.
Reduce MotionSheets and panels appear without sliding. Status stays as text, and every action stays available (R-MOT-4).

Where it lives
Route: the same as desktop (/p/:project/:stage). Width decides the layout through container queries, not the user agent. Below 768 px free-position canvas editing is not offered (R-WS-4).UI (TS/React): OpenDesign responsive layout (Apache-2.0, e033aad) for stacked panels and sheets; Momo (no licence) drawer, hover:none branch and safe-area insets as patterns only. Frame list, viewer, review bar and hand-off list are Spekin components over the same core calls as desktop.Core (Rust): the same review, revision and manifest calls as desktop. The hand-off snapshot is built once and pinned (R-WS-8).Open for Enrico: bottom tab bar (drawn) or top tabs as in the wireframe · whether Style is read-only on a phone · tablet landscape (drawn portrait only).
