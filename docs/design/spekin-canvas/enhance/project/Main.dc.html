CANVASSPEKIN · ENHANCE PROMPT · BY YRAVG

Spekin: an Enhance prompt button in the chat composer

A small sparkle button left of Send that rewrites the user's draft into a clearer, fuller prompt before it is sent. One board, S41, with six states: empty draft, draft ready, enhancing, review proposal, applied with Undo, and blocked at the credit cap. Same look, kit and sample project (Meranti Yard) as the main canvas.

Board
S41 · Enhance prompt · F531 to F539
Why
Short or rough drafts waste a paid run. Enhance spends about 1 to 2 credits to make the message complete before an 18 to 30 credit run.It stays safe: the result is a proposal you review, nothing is sent for you, one Undo restores your exact words, and it invents no requirement (guesses are marked assumptions).
